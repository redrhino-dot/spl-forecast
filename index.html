Pred. Score (probability) is the single most likely exact scoreline across the whole grid — this can land on a draw even when Home or Away is the favored outcome overall, since a draw's probability concentrates on 1-2 cells (0-0, 1-1) while a win's is spread across a dozen scorelines.
Pred. Score (fav result) is the top scoreline within whichever of Home/Draw/Away the model actually favors — cross-check against the Model Home/Draw/Away columns to see which category that is.
VALUE marks an underdog (market odds ≥ 2.50) where the model rates that outcome at least 6 points more likely than the market's implied probability.
